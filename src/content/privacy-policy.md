## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Founders Hook ("we", "our", "us"). Founders Hook is an AI-powered platform that helps users discover and understand startups by analysing publicly accessible startup websites and generating structured insights about their business models, problems, and solutions. The platform also allows users to create profiles, connect with founders and builders, apply to startup roles, and communicate privately.

Founders Hook is operated by three individual founders based in India and is currently not registered as a separate legal entity.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and how you can exercise control over your information when using [https://foundershook.in](https://foundershook.in).

By using Founders Hook, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information that users voluntarily provide when registering for or using Founders Hook.

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Name
- Username
- Email address
- Password (stored in hashed form using bcrypt)
- Optional mobile number
- Email verification status
- Early-access status and invite code, where applicable

We use this information to create and manage user accounts, authenticate users, send verification and account-related emails, and provide access to our services.

### 2.2 Profile Information

Users may optionally provide or upload:

- Profile pictures
- Profile banner images
- A short biography
- Skills
- Onboarding answers collected through our profile setup flow
- Followers and following relationships

Names, usernames, profile pictures, banners, biographies, skills and follower/following counts displayed on user profiles may be publicly accessible to visitors, including people who do not have a Founders Hook account.

### 2.3 Startup Information

When users submit a startup website for analysis or create a startup profile, we may collect:

- Submitted website URLs
- Information extracted from publicly accessible website pages
- Startup details provided by users, such as name, tagline, description, category, icon, cover image, open roles and team members
- AI-generated startup descriptions and analyses

This information is used to generate startup insights and make the resulting analyses available through Founders Hook. Startup profiles and analyses are publicly accessible to anyone, including visitors who are not registered users. Users should avoid submitting confidential information or information they do not have permission to share.

### 2.4 Application Information

When you apply to a role listed on a startup, we collect the information you submit, which may include:

- Name
- Email address
- Mobile number
- Gender, where provided
- Experience
- Resume (uploaded as a PDF)
- A message to the startup
- Application status

Application details are stored in our databases and shared with the relevant startup founder or team through the messaging functionality. Please do not include sensitive information in applications or messages that you do not want shared with the recipient.

### 2.5 Messaging and Conversation Information

Founders Hook allows users to communicate privately with other users. Messages, conversation metadata and related details (such as participant names, usernames, profile pictures and application cards) are stored using Firebase. Conversations are intended to be accessible only to their participants through the application. However, authorised individuals with appropriate administrative access to the underlying Firebase project may be able to access stored data.

Users can delete individual messages and delete entire conversations through the available messaging functionality.

### 2.6 Community Content

Users may create or interact with content in our Knowledge Hub, including posts, comments and replies. Posts are stored in our primary database, while comments and replies are stored using Firebase. Knowledge Hub posts, comments and replies are publicly accessible.

### 2.7 Notifications

We store in-app notifications relating to applications, account activity and platform updates so that they can be displayed to you.

### 2.8 Technical and Security Information

We collect limited technical information as part of operating and securing the platform:

- IP address and browser user-agent information in security logs for authentication and account-security events
- Short-lived IP-based counters used for rate limiting
- Essential authentication cookies and tokens

We do not use IP addresses or device information for advertising, analytics or behavioural profiling.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

- Creating and managing user accounts.
- Authenticating users through our JWT-based authentication system.
- Sending verification, password-reset and account-security emails.
- Displaying user profiles and networking information.
- Allowing users to follow one another and discover potential collaborators.
- Allowing users to communicate with one another.
- Processing job applications and sharing them with the relevant startup.
- Scraping publicly accessible startup websites.
- Processing extracted website information through AI services.
- Powering our AI onboarding and profile-building assistant.
- Generating startup analyses and making them publicly accessible.
- Maintaining and improving the functionality of Founders Hook.
- Sending in-app notifications.
- Responding to user enquiries and privacy requests.
- Maintaining the security and reliability of our platform, including rate limiting and abuse prevention.
- Complying with applicable legal requirements.

We do not currently sell users' personal information.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Data Storage

Founders Hook relies on third-party services to operate its platform. These services may process information on our behalf in accordance with their respective terms and privacy policies.

### 4.1 MongoDB

We use MongoDB to store:

- User account information (including name, username, email, mobile number, hashed password and verification status).
- User profile details (including biography, skills, onboarding answers and social relationships).
- Startup information and AI-generated analyses.
- Application-related data, including resumes, gender, mobile number and experience where provided.
- Knowledge Hub posts.
- In-app notifications.
- One-time verification and password-reset records.

### 4.2 Cloudinary

We use Cloudinary to store and deliver uploaded images, including profile pictures, profile banners, startup icons and startup cover images.

When you delete your account, we attempt to delete the images we can associate with your account from Cloudinary. Cached or backed-up copies may persist for a limited period in accordance with Cloudinary's practices.

### 4.3 Firebase

We use Firebase for the following purposes:

- Cloud Firestore is used to store messages, conversation metadata and application cards exchanged between users.
- Cloud Firestore is also used to store Knowledge Hub comments and replies.
- Firebase Authentication is used to authenticate users for real-time messaging using custom tokens minted by our servers and linked to your Founders Hook account.

Firestore security rules restrict access to conversations and messages to their participants. Certain content, such as Knowledge Hub comments and replies, is publicly readable.

### 4.4 Vercel

Our website and application are hosted on Vercel. Vercel may process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser information and server logs, as part of hosting, security and infrastructure operations.

Although Founders Hook does not use IP addresses or device information for analytics or profiling, we do record IP address and browser user-agent information in security logs for authentication and abuse-prevention purposes, and infrastructure providers may automatically process certain technical information.

### 4.5 OpenRouter

We use the OpenRouter API to access AI models. Information may be sent through OpenRouter to the selected AI model provider for processing in the following situations:

- When a user submits a startup website for analysis, information extracted from the website is sent to generate startup descriptions, explanations and analyses.
- When a user uses our AI onboarding or profile-building assistant, information provided during that conversation (such as name, role, industry, experience, biography, skills and chat messages) is sent to generate or improve the user's profile.

OpenRouter and the selected model providers may process information under their respective privacy policies and data-handling terms.

### 4.6 Brevo

We use Brevo to send transactional emails, including account verification codes, password-reset codes, password-reset confirmations and security codes for changing account settings. Your email address may be shared with Brevo for this purpose. We currently do not use Brevo for promotional or marketing email campaigns.

### 4.7 Other Providers and External Links

We load some images and fonts from third-party content-delivery and font providers, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Users may also choose to share Google Meet links through the messaging functionality; use of Google Meet is subject to Google's own terms and privacy policy.

## 5. Publicly Accessible Information

Certain information on Founders Hook is publicly accessible. This includes:

- User names, usernames and profile pictures displayed on public profiles.
- Profile banners, biographies, skills and follower/following counts.
- Startup profiles, team members and AI-generated startup analyses.
- Knowledge Hub posts, comments and replies.

Anyone may access publicly available profiles, startup content and Knowledge Hub content, including visitors who do not have an account. Users should not upload confidential, sensitive or private information to publicly accessible areas of Founders Hook.

Email addresses, mobile numbers and resumes are not displayed on public profiles. Application details are shared only with the relevant startup through the application and messaging functionality, subject to the retention described in Section 7.

## 6. Private Messaging

Messages exchanged between users are intended to remain private between the participants. However, these messages are stored in Firebase and may be accessible to authorised administrators who have appropriate access to the underlying infrastructure.

Users can delete individual messages and delete entire conversations through the available messaging functionality. Deleting a conversation removes it and its messages for all participants.

Founders Hook does not currently provide built-in message reporting or user-blocking functionality. Users can contact us directly at [foundershook@gmail.com](mailto:foundershook@gmail.com) regarding concerns about inappropriate behaviour or privacy.

## 7. Account Deletion and Data Removal

Users may permanently delete their accounts through the account deletion functionality available in account settings. Upon account deletion, we aim to remove the associated account information, profile information, uploaded profile images, startup data, applications, posts, notifications and verification records from our active systems.

**Important exceptions — messages and conversations:** Messages and conversations previously exchanged with other users are not automatically deleted when an account is removed. This includes conversation history and any details shared within messages, such as application cards and resume links. The other participant may continue to see messages from the deleted account. In such cases, the deleted user's identity will no longer be available as an active Founders Hook account, and the remaining participant may be prompted: "This user no longer exists. Delete conversation?" The remaining participant can then decide whether to delete the conversation.

**Knowledge Hub content:** Posts you created are removed from our primary database when your account is deleted. Comments and replies you posted in the Knowledge Hub are stored in Firebase and are not automatically deleted when you close your account; you can delete them individually before deleting your account.

Some information may temporarily remain in technical logs or backups, or be retained where required by applicable law. We will handle such information in accordance with applicable retention and deletion requirements.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored and processed through Founders Hook. Our security practices include:

- Password hashing using bcrypt.
- JWT-based session authentication using signed, HTTP-only cookies.
- Firebase custom authentication tokens linked to your Founders Hook account.
- Firestore security rules that restrict conversation access to participants.
- Rate limiting on authentication and sensitive endpoints.
- Using established third-party infrastructure providers.
- Restricting administrative access to application databases and services.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users should maintain the confidentiality of their login credentials and notify us if they suspect unauthorised access to their accounts.

## 9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Founders Hook currently does not use third-party analytics or behavioural tracking services. We use:

- An essential session cookie required to keep you signed in.
- Browser storage used by Firebase Authentication to maintain your messaging session.
- Local browser storage for interface preferences.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, or additional tracking technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and implement any required consent or preference mechanisms.

## 12. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the services. Specific retention practices include:

- One-time verification and password-reset codes expire automatically after a short period.
- Account, profile, startup and application data are retained until you delete the relevant content or delete your account.
- Messages and conversations are retained until they are deleted by a participant or otherwise removed in accordance with this Policy.
- Security logs and backups may be retained for a limited period for security, fraud-prevention and legal purposes.
