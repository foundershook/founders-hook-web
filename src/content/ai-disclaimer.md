## 1. Introduction

Founders Hook uses artificial intelligence (AI) technologies to assist with the creation and presentation of startup information on its platform.

This AI Disclaimer explains how AI-generated content is used, its limitations, and the responsibilities of users and Founders Hook.

This document should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

## 2. How Our AI System Works

When a founder registers a startup on Founders Hook, they are asked to provide their company's website URL.

Our platform then:

1. Extracts publicly accessible information from the submitted website using web scraping technologies.
2. Processes the extracted information through third-party AI services.
3. Generates three sections:
   - **Description:** A general explanation of what the startup does.
   - **Problem:** The problem the startup aims to solve.
   - **Solution:** How the startup attempts to address that problem.

These sections are generated using the information extracted from the submitted website and the AI model's output.

## 3. Automatic Publication of AI-Generated Content

AI-generated startup descriptions, problem statements and solutions are automatically published on Founders Hook once the generation process is completed.

Prior manual approval by the founder is not required.

Founders can subsequently review, edit and update all three sections through the available platform functionality.

## 4. Accuracy and Limitations

AI-generated content is produced automatically and may contain:

- Inaccurate or incomplete descriptions.

- Missing details about a startup's products or services.
- Outdated information.
- Incorrect assumptions based on the extracted content.

Founders Hook does not independently verify every statement generated by its AI system.

We do not guarantee that AI-generated content will always be accurate, complete, reliable or up to date.
